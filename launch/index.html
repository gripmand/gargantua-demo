What it does

Alpha attribution

Every closed trade is scored against the market over its own holding window. You see skill and beta as separate lines, per trade and in aggregate.

Expectancy that survives scrutiny

Win rate is theater. You get expectancy per setup with effective N and t-stat attached, so a "great" setup with nine samples is labelled as what it is.

Setup grading

Each entry carries its conditions — trend quality, VIX, extension from the 200, credit state. Grades let you find out which conditions actually pay you.

An edge registry

A standing record of every setup you've tested and its dated verdict. Validated, marginal, thin, killed. Nothing gets traded on vibes twice.
